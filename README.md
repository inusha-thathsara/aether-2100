## 📖 Overview
Aether 2100 is a unified, next-generation digital mobility platform designed for smart cities in the year 2100. It seamlessly orchestrates four autonomous transit modes into a single, cohesive user experience:
1. **Autonomous Mag-Bus:** Level 5 street transit powered by continuous surface induction rails (95 km/h).
2. **Hyper-Tube Maglev:** Subterranean and elevated vacuum-tube magnetic levitation corridors (720 km/h).
3. **eVTOL Sky-Pods:** 6-passenger vertical takeoff aerial shuttles navigating dynamic 3D holographic flight corridors (240 km/h).
4. **Smart-Road Glide Pods:** On-demand micro-capsules powered wirelessly through nano-induction roadways (120 km/h).

---

## ♿ Inclusive Accessibility & Universal Design
Cre8x 3.0 heavily evaluates genuine accessibility (15 pts accessibility + 20 pts usability). Aether 2100 provides:
- **Plain-Language Mode:** Automatically translates technical transit jargon into reassuring, easy-to-follow steps for elderly and first-time travelers.
- **High-Contrast Vision Mode (WCAG AAA):** Pure black canvas with high-visibility solar yellow accents and bold borders.
- **Audio Feedback & Voice Guidance:** Web Audio API synthesized harmonic chimes and Text-to-Speech vocal stops.
- **Emergency Holo-Link (SOS):** Instant connection to a 24/7 human transit officer for disoriented passengers.

---

## 📱 The 3 Mandatory Screens
- **Screen 1 (Discovery & Home):** Multi-modal transit mode selector, Quantum Route Planner, Oracle AI Predictive advisory card, and instant hub presets.
- **Screen 2 (Journey Details):** Multi-modal itinerary timeline, critical transfer gates/platforms callout, step-free elevator instructions, and live delay telemetry.
- **Screen 3 (Live Map Tracking HUD):** Real-time vector map with animated vehicle position and radar wave pulses, speedometer, altitude, inductive grid sync, and the **Plain-Language Status Card** specifically tailored for non-map users.
- **Bonus Feature:** Universal Biometric Boarding Pass with dynamic holographic QR verification for participant `IT Gunasekara`.
